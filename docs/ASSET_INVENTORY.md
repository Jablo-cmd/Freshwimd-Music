(generated by `python3 scripts/prepare-images.py`) and are registered with alt text in `content/images.ts`.

person at the console (the owner should confirm they are Fresh Wind Music's studio and that they are happy for the
person pictured to appear). The Pinterest screenshots, the "Images may be subject to copyright" screenshot and the
other Google-image screenshots are **third-party / unattributed**. They are used on the site only as atmosphere
(service cards, banners), never captioned as Fresh Wind Music's facilities, and should be **replaced with original
photography or have licensing confirmed before launch**.
